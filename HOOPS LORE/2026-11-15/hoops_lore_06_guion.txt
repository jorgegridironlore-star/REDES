Down eight with thirty-five seconds left, one man scored thirteen points by himself and won the game.
December ninth, two thousand four. Toyota Center, Houston, Texas.
The Rockets host the San Antonio Spurs.
Thirty-five seconds to go. Spurs lead seventy-six to sixty-eight.
Some fans are already heading for the exits.
Tracy McGrady pulls up from deep. Three! Seventy-six, seventy-one.
The Spurs answer with two free throws. Seventy-eight, seventy-one.
Twenty-four seconds left. McGrady again. Three, and the foul!
He makes the free throw. Seventy-eight, seventy-five.
Tim Duncan hits two free throws. Eighty, seventy-five.
Eleven seconds left. McGrady, right over Bruce Bowen. Three! Eighty, seventy-eight.
The Spurs inbound. Devin Brown slips, and loses the ball.
McGrady grabs it and races up the court.
He pulls up from twenty-seven feet... Good! With one point seven seconds left.
Eighty-one, eighty. Rockets win.
Four threes and a free throw. Thirteen points in thirty-five seconds.
After the game, McGrady had a message: for all those fans that left, you missed a great game.
