With eight minutes left, the Giants led by twenty-one points. They still lost.
December nineteenth, twenty ten. The New Meadowlands, New Jersey.
Giants against Eagles.
Fourth quarter, eight seventeen to play. Giants thirty-one, Eagles ten.
Then Michael Vick hit Brent Celek for a sixty-five-yard touchdown.
Next, an onside kick. Philadelphia got it back.
Vick ran in another touchdown himself.
Then he found Jeremy Maclin. Thirty-one to thirty-one.
Fourteen seconds left. The Giants had to punt.
The order: kick it out of bounds.
But the snap came in high, and rookie Matt Dodge hit a low line drive, straight to DeSean Jackson.
Jackson bobbled it at his own thirty-five.
He picked it up, and took off.
Midfield. The forty. The thirty. Nobody could catch him.
At the goal line, he didn't go in. He danced along it, until the clock showed all zeros.
Then he stepped into the end zone. Eagles thirty-eight, Giants thirty-one.
Twenty-eight points in the last seven and a half minutes.
On the sideline, Tom Coughlin threw his notes and went after his punter.
It was the first punt return touchdown to end a game in NFL history.
